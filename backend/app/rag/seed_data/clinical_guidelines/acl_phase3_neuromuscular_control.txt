ACL Reconstruction Rehabilitation: Phase 3 - Neuromuscular Control and Running Progression (Months 3-5)

Entry criteria for this phase: full, pain-free range of motion; no effusion; quadriceps strength at least 80% of the contralateral limb; and a normal walking gait without compensation. Jogging progressions typically begin on a treadmill or track in this window, starting with short, low-intensity intervals.

Plyometric introduction should be gradual and criteria-based rather than time-based: a patient should demonstrate good single-leg landing mechanics (minimal dynamic valgus at the knee) on double-leg jump-landing tasks before progressing to single-leg hop tasks.

A pain flare or new effusion after a running or plyometric session is not automatically a setback, but a repeated pattern of swelling after activity across two or more consecutive sessions should prompt a temporary reduction in load and closer monitoring, since it may indicate the graft or joint is not yet ready for that load level.
