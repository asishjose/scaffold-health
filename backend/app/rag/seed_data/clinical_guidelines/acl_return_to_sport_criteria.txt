ACL Reconstruction Rehabilitation: Return-to-Sport Criteria (Month 6+)

Return-to-sport clearance is criteria-based, not purely time-based, though most protocols consider month 9 the earliest reasonable target for cutting/pivoting sports given graft maturation timelines. Commonly used criteria include: quadriceps and hamstring limb symmetry index of at least 90% on formal or functional testing, single-leg hop test symmetry of at least 90% across multiple hop variations, and no reported episodes of the knee "giving way" during the preceding month of progressive loading.

Psychological readiness is also a meaningful factor; a patient who reports persistent fear of re-injury or explicitly avoids loading the operated leg during functional testing should have return-to-sport timing revisited with the full care team rather than cleared on physical criteria alone.

Clearance is always a therapist and surgeon decision made collaboratively; no automated system should generate a return-to-sport clearance recommendation on its own.
