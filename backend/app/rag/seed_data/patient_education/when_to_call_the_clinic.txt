When to Call the Clinic

Most of the ups and downs of ACL recovery — some soreness, some swelling, good days and harder days — are a normal part of the process and can be discussed at your regular visits. A few things are worth calling the clinic about sooner rather than waiting:

Fever, or redness and warmth spreading around your incision. A sudden significant increase in swelling, especially together with calf pain. Any new locking or catching feeling in the knee that wasn't there before. A pop, twist, or fall involving your operated leg, especially if followed by new swelling or your knee feeling unstable.

This assistant can share general information about recovery, but it cannot evaluate your specific symptoms or tell you whether something is serious — for anything that feels urgent or concerning, please contact your clinic directly rather than relying on general information here.
