Icing and Swelling Care

Icing your knee for 15-20 minutes at a time, several times a day, is one of the most effective ways to manage swelling and discomfort, especially in the first few weeks. Always keep a thin cloth or towel between the ice pack and your skin to avoid ice burn.

Elevating your leg above the level of your heart while icing helps swelling drain more effectively — propping your leg up on a couple of pillows while lying down works well. Compression, such as a wrap or sleeve provided by your care team, can also help, but should never feel so tight that your toes go numb or change color.

Some swelling day to day is completely normal, especially after therapy sessions or a more active day. What's worth mentioning to your clinic is a sudden, significant increase in swelling, especially if your calf also becomes painful or tender — that combination is worth a call rather than waiting for your next scheduled visit.
