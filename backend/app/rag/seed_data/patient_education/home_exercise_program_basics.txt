Getting the Most Out of Your Home Exercise Program

Your therapist will give you a specific set of exercises to do between visits — consistency matters more than intensity, especially early on. Doing your exercises a little bit most days generally works better than doing a long session once a week.

Some muscle soreness after exercises, especially when you're increasing what you're doing, is a normal part of the process. A good general guide is that mild soreness which settles within a day is expected, while sharp pain during an exercise, or swelling that clearly increases after a session and doesn't calm down with rest and ice, is worth mentioning to your therapist.

If an exercise on your sheet doesn't make sense, or you're not sure you're doing it correctly, it's always better to ask at your next visit (or call the clinic) than to guess — doing an exercise with the wrong form can slow your progress rather than help it.
