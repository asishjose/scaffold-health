Using Your Brace and Crutches

Your surgeon or therapist will tell you exactly how long you need to wear your brace and how much weight you're allowed to put on your leg — this varies depending on your specific surgery, so don't compare your instructions to a friend's or something you read online.

When walking with crutches, move both crutches forward together, then step through with your operated leg, following whatever weight-bearing instructions you were given. Go slowly, especially on stairs or uneven ground, and don't hesitate to ask a family member for a hand during this early period.

Most patients transition off crutches gradually as their therapist clears them for more weight-bearing and their walking pattern becomes steady without a limp. If you feel like you're being asked to put more weight on the leg than feels safe, that's a good thing to bring up with your therapist rather than pushing through on your own.
