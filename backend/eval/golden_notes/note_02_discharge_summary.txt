DISCHARGE SUMMARY

Procedure: Arthroscopic ACL reconstruction, right knee, using autologous
patellar tendon graft.

Post-operative instructions: Patient is to remain non-weight-bearing on the
right lower extremity for the first two weeks following surgery. A hinged
knee brace locked in full extension must be worn at all times, including
while sleeping, until the follow-up appointment.

Patient tolerated the procedure well and was discharged home in stable
condition with crutches and a cryotherapy unit.
