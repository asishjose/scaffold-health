RADIOLOGY REPORT — MRI RIGHT KNEE WITHOUT CONTRAST

CLINICAL HISTORY: 24-year-old recreational soccer player with acute right knee
injury sustained during a pivoting maneuver. Positive Lachman test on exam.

FINDINGS: Complete full-thickness tear of the anterior cruciate ligament (ACL)
with associated bone marrow edema at the lateral femoral condyle. Menisci are
intact. No evidence of collateral ligament injury.

IMPRESSION: Complete ACL rupture. Findings are consistent with the clinical
diagnosis of ACL reconstruction candidacy. Recommend orthopedic surgery
referral.
